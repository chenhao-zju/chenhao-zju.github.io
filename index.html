Welcome! My name is Hao Chen. I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. Song Guo (IEEE Fellow, Chair Professor). I previously obtained my M.S. from Zhejiang University and B.S. from Shandong University. I am also affiliated with the Jindouyun (筋斗云) Program at ByteDance and Project Up (青云计划) at Youtu, Tencent.

My research interests include Post-Training, On-Policy Distillation (OPD), Vision-Language Models, Mixture-of-Experts, Computer Vision, and AI for Weather. I serve as a reviewer for top journals and conferences, such as TIP, TCSVT, ACM MM, and IJCAI.
